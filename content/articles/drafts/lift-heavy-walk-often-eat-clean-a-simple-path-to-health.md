---
title: "Lift Heavy, Walk Often, Eat Clean: A Simple Path to Health"
---

Three times a week, hit at least one of the squat, bench, deadlift, and overhead press, pushing 3-5 heavy reps in linear progression. Walk 10,000 steps daily, spread over the day. Eat clean foods. Aim for protein every meal. No processed foods.

Beast Over Burden Podcast: ["The Wonders of Walking: The Benefits of Walking for Your Health"](https://youtu.be/cejdBCcfryE?si=L_sF3x4DjU4wUDpZ)
