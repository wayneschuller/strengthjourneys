Being called a *weirdo* often carries a negative connotation, but for those who are deeply passionate about their interests, it can be a badge of honor. [Guan Un describes a weirdo](https://find.yourweirdos.com/posts/there-and-back-again) as: "*someone who loves something deeply and seeks to share that love with others*." For barbell weirdos, that passion is for lifting—not just exercise, but everything about [barbells](https://www.strengthjourneys.xyz/articles/what-barbell-should-i-start-with), plates, and the gym atmosphere.

Barbell weirdos are the ones who savor every rep, appreciate the feel of [cold iron](https://www.strengthjourneys.xyz/articles/the-iron-and-the-soul-author-henry-rollins) in their hands, and value the [small, tangible progress](https://www.strengthjourneys.xyz/articles/what-are-barbells-good-for) they make over time. It’s not just about reaching fitness goals; it’s about the process itself. [Every session](https://www.strengthjourneys.xyz/log) becomes more than a workout—it’s a craft.

At [Strength Journeys](https://www.strengthjourneys.xyz/), we embrace that love. Our app is designed for those who don’t just lift but genuinely enjoy the entire experience. You’ll feel right at home here if you're a barbell weirdo.

This isn’t about trends or fitness fads. It's about embracing a genuine love for strength, growth, and the tools that help us achieve these goals.
