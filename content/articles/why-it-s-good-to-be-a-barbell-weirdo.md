Being called a *weirdo* often carries a negative connotation, but for those who are deeply passionate about their interests, it can be a badge of honor. [Guan Un describes a weirdo](https://find.yourweirdos.com/posts/there-and-back-again) as: "*someone who loves something deeply and seeks to share that love with others*." For barbell weirdos, that passion is for lifting—not just exercise, but everything about barbells, plates, and the gym atmosphere.

Barbell weirdos are the ones who savor every rep, appreciate the feel of cold iron in their hands, and value the small, tangible progress they make over time. It’s not just about reaching fitness goals; it’s about the process itself. Every session becomes more than a workout—it’s a craft.

At [Strength Journeys](https://www.strengthjourneys.xyz/), we embrace that love. Our app is designed for those who don’t just lift but genuinely enjoy the entire experience. You’ll feel right at home here if you're a barbell weirdo.

This isn’t about trends or fitness fads. It's about embracing a genuine love for strength, growth, and the tools that help us achieve these goals.
