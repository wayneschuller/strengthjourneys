---
title: "Strength ratings much improved"
---

We now chart your position against our strength standards much more reliably.

We also put in an e1rm marker if you have an e1rm higher than your best actual lift.

[![Strength standards sliders with an E1RM marker beyond the best actual lift](/changelog/2025-10-10-2/image-1.webp)](/strength-levels)

The chart will show your best actual lift, even if it is a higher rep scheme.

Thanks to athlete Tom Clark who reported this bug 8 months ago.
