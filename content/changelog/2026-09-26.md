Updates from September 15 to September 26.

### How Strong Am I? Is Now Your Strength Story

How Strong Am I? reads like a sentence about you now: "I am a 30 year-old male weighing 200lb. My bench press is 155lb." Drag the sliders and the story, the rings and your percentile all move together, with no boxes to type into.

The rings are bigger and count up as they fill. Each group unlocks in turn, from the general population through gym-goers and barbell lifters to powerlifting culture, once you clear the ring outside it. Share a link and whoever opens it sees your ratings straight away, and a total close to 1000lb links through to the 1000lb Club calculator.

![How Strong Am I? strength story with all four rings unlocked](/changelog/2026-09-26/image-1.webp "Twelve years of lifting, told as a story")

### Pick Your AI Coach

The AI Lifting Assistant has a model switcher in the chat header. GPT-6 Luna is the new default, and Grok 4.20 is there for everyone too. GPT-5.6 Luna and GPT-6 Sol are one Google sign-in away. The switcher also shows which prompt version your coach is running, and a copied answer carries a source line naming the model and prompt version.

![The AI model switcher open in the chat header](/changelog/2026-09-26/image-2.webp "Pick your coach from the chat header")

### A Coach That Sees More of Your Training

The coach now reads your last 20 sessions, with up to 12 sets per lift from your latest one, so a PR set is never hidden behind its warm-ups. It also knows how many sessions you have done this month for each main lift, so "review my month" gets the whole month.

Answers read better too. Every lift with a guide is linked the first time it comes up, tables render properly again, and follow-up suggestions arrive faster. Give any reply a thumbs up or down and it helps shape the next prompt version.

### Ask the AI About Any Chart, Then Share It

Progress charts, tonnage charts and the Long Game card each have one compact control to ask the AI about exactly what that chart shows, copy it as text, or copy it as an image. Copied images leave the buttons behind and carry a small Strength Journeys signature, ready to post.

![Back squat estimated one rep max chart with AI review and copy image buttons](/changelog/2026-09-26/image-3.webp "AI review and Copy image sit under every progress chart")

### Charts You Can Read With a Tap

Hover or tap a point on a progress chart and the tooltip shows the sessions behind it, without leaving the page. On a touchscreen a tap is for reading, and the session opens only when you ask. Fresh records say Today or Yesterday and get a tint, so a new PR stands out.

On lift guides, the strength circles reveal one ring at a time as you cross each threshold, so there is always a next one to chase.

### Three More Lifts, and Variations That Count

The push press, sumo deadlift and paused bench press have their own drawings. Variations now count toward your Big Four rows in The Month in Iron: a front squat fills the squat row and a rack pull adds half its tonnage, and each row says which variations it includes.

![The Month in Iron with a squat row that includes front squats](/changelog/2026-09-26/image-4.webp "Front squats now count toward the squat row")

### Articles With a Fresh Look

The article library opens with a photo-led featured section, and every article has a new editorial layout: a big title, the cover image full width, captioned figures, pull quotes, a reading progress bar and related articles picked by topic.

### Importing

- FitNotes exports can be imported, in kilograms or pounds.
- Real StrongLifts 5x5 exports are read correctly, dates in the right order.
- Every importer reads decimal commas, so 82,5 is 82.5.
- Your own sheet copes with common typos: a stray apostrophe, "g" for kg, or a weight range is read the way you meant it, and rows logging a run or a body measurement are left out instead of becoming pounds.
- Dumbbell and machine sets stay out of the Big Four.
- The import page loads instantly instead of showing a spinner.
