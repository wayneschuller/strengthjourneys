Updates from October 18 to October 26.

### Strength Unwrapped

[Strength Unwrapped](/strength-year-in-review) is your year of lifting as a story of eleven cards, and this year it has been rebuilt from the ground up.

[![Strength Unwrapped recap cards fanned out: lifter type, training calendar and strength gains](/changelog/2026-10-26/image-1.webp "Eleven cards, made to share")](/strength-year-in-review)

- **Your lifter type.** Are you The Metronome, The Heavy Hitter, The Competitor or The Explorer? Your year decides, with your favourite training day, your go-to set and how many lifts you trained behind it.
- **Strength gains.** Your best estimated max on the squat, bench, deadlift and press, set beside last year's, with a badge on every lift that climbed.
- **A training calendar.** Twelve little months with every session shaded by how much you did, your meet days in gold, and your busiest month up top.
- **One card for the whole year.** Your lifter type, sessions, tonnage, lifetime PRs, best streak and busiest month together, for when you post just one.
- **Share the whole deck.** One tap saves every card as a story-sized image, and on a phone they go straight to the share sheet together.

The numbers count up as each card arrives, every card glows in your theme's colours, and a tap on either side moves you through the story. Your name goes on the title card when you are signed in.

From December 1 a banner across the top of the site will show your session count and lifetime PRs, with your lifter type hidden until you open it.

### A Livelier AI Coach

The [AI Lifting Assistant](/ai-lifting-assistant) is now a full-screen chat. It opens on a greeting from a little robot coach who blinks, glances at the question you hover and hops when you pick one. Once you are talking, the conversation fills the window with the message box pinned at the bottom.

- Answers read better: sets show as chips like 3x5@100kg, milestones get a highlighter stroke, a closing tip becomes a takeaway card, and a date you trained on links straight to that day in your log.
- Fresh PR in your latest session? The coach greets you with a trophy and offers to review it with you. Lifters with a few years of data get their 12-month bests celebrated too.
- Hover a suggestion and it types itself into the message box, ready to edit or send.
- The coach now reads the notes you write on your sets.
- Claude Haiku 5.5 is the new default model, and it answers faster with fuller replies. DeepSeek moves up to V4.1 Flash.

### A Log Made for Thumbs

[The log](/log) is much easier to use one-handed on a phone.

- Every set has its own card, with bigger numbers.
- Reps, weight and notes are larger fields, and adding or editing a set uses one panel with big Cancel and Save buttons that stay above the keyboard.
- Swipe sideways to move between sessions. The session follows your finger and the next one slides in.
- Past sessions say how long ago they were: yesterday, 3 weeks ago and so on.
- On Android your phone gives a light buzz when a set reaches your sheet, and a bigger one for a PR.

### Under the Hood

- If Google keeps refusing to save a set, the log hands it back to you with a clear message instead of retrying quietly.
- The unit reminder now looks only at your own sheet, so kilogram lifters are no longer told their sheet is in pounds.

Keep lifting!
