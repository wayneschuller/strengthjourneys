Updates from October 27 to November 28.

### Bodyweight Tracking

Your bodyweight now lives right beside your lifts, on its own [Bodyweight](/bodyweight) page. This is a first version, and it will get plenty more polish in the coming weeks. We would love to hear how you use it and what you want from it, so [send us your ideas](/changelog#feature-request).

- **Log a weigh-in on any session.** It goes into your sheet as a Bodyweight row, alongside your sets, and syncs like any other entry.
- **See the trend.** The Bodyweight page charts every weigh-in, and you can edit one in place. The week card on your dashboard shows a little sparkline and offers to take today's weight when your last one is getting old.
- **Strength for your size.** The [visualizer](/visualizer) can chart your bodyweight and your strength relative to it. Strength ratings and the percentile-over-time chart now score each lift at what you weighed that day, so a cut or a bulk is measured fairly.
- **StrongLifts weigh-ins come across.** Importing a StrongLifts export brings your recorded bodyweight with it.

![The Bodyweight page: weigh-ins charted, and strength for your bodyweight by month](/changelog/2026-11-28/image-1.webp "Your weigh-ins, and your strength for your size")

### Smarter Set Suggestions

The suggested sets on [the log](/log) now learn from how you actually train, and we tested them by replaying years of a real training log.

- **Your own warm-up ramp.** Warm-ups follow the ramp you used last time, at the weights you used, so you can just tap through them.
- **Back-off sets.** Once your heavy work is done, the log offers your usual back-offs, scaled to today's top set.
- **Your usual opener.** Each lift opens with the first set you most often start with, so one light day does not set the tone for the next.
- **Coming back from a break.** After time away from a lift, the top-set target builds back over a few sessions, with your old best still on offer if you are ready for it.
- **Every option, equal.** Each suggestion is a clear button to press, with an "Add custom set" button beside them for anything else.

The date header now stays pinned at the top as you scroll through a session.

### Artwork in Every Theme

Every lift drawing is now redrawn to follow your chosen theme, from Blueprint to Starry Night, where the lifts get painted swirls and gold stars. The box squat and incline bench press have new drawings too, and you can search every lift from the top of the log.

![The squat, bench press, deadlift and strict press drawings in four themes](/changelog/2026-11-28/image-2.webp "Squat in Starry Night, bench in Blueprint, deadlift in Evergreen, press in Retro Arcade")

### A Handier AI Coach

- Copy any reply from the [AI Lifting Assistant](/ai-lifting-assistant) with one tap.
- The opening suggestions are fresh on every visit, and "More ideas" swaps the whole set for new ones.

### Smoother Charts

- The visualizer keeps your history steady when you change the time range, and its tooltip glides between sizes.
- Progress guide charts mark your latest session.
- The Month in Iron now shows a lineup of your own main lifts, and its monthly pace restarts from the day you come back after a layoff.

### Under the Hood

- Logs that mix kilograms and pounds now compare and rate every set correctly.
- Fitbod exports from pound lifters import in pounds.
- Switching sheets is clearer: the app tells you when your connected sheet is the most complete one it found, and cancelling the Drive picker takes you back to the sheet chooser.
- The Long Game heatmap labels its years correctly in every time zone.
- Returning lifters load faster, since your sheet is now read once instead of twice.

Keep lifting!
